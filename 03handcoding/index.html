Why Pizza Is the Best Food Ever

Pizza is one of the most loved foods in the world. You can put almost any topping on it and it still tastes amazing.

Whether you like thin crust or thick crust, cheese or no cheese, there is a pizza for everyone. Making pizza at home is also fun and easy.

Popular Pizza Toppings

  • Pepperoni
  • Mushrooms
  • Extra Cheese
  • Bell Peppers
  • Olives

Steps to Make a Basic Pizza

  1. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C)
  2. Roll out your pizza dough
  3. Add tomato sauce and cheese
  4. Add your favorite toppings
  5. Bake for 10-15 minutes

Pizza Terms You Should Know

Margherita
A classic pizza with tomato, mozzarella, and fresh basil.
Neapolitan
Thin crust pizza that originated in Naples, Italy.
Wood-Fired
Pizza cooked in a very hot brick oven using wood.
