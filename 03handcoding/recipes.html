Classic Pepperoni Pizza

This is the most popular pizza in America. It's simple but so tasty. Use spicy pepperoni for extra flavor.

To make it perfect, put the pepperoni on top of the cheese so it gets crispy in the oven.

Ingredients You'll Need

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni slices
  • Olive oil

Cooking Instructions

  1. Let dough sit at room temperature for 20 minutes
  2. Stretch the dough into a circle
  3. Spread sauce evenly on the dough
  4. Sprinkle cheese all over
  5. Layer pepperoni on top
  6. Bake at 475°F for 12 minutes

More Pizza Vocabulary

Stromboli
