Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship

Digital citizenship is the concept of using technology, the internet, and digital platforms responsibly, ethically, and safely. It's about how people interact online, understand their rights and responsibilities, and make smart decisions in the digital world.

What is digital law?

Digital law is a part of digital citizenship that refers to the rules and legal responsibilities for using technology, the internet, and digital content. It's essentially about what's legal and illegal online.

what is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy.

It's a set of rules or guidelines that an organization creates to tell users how they are allowed to use its digital resources

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is using someone else's words, ideas, or work without giving them credit and presenting it as your own.

What is Academic Dishonerty?

Academic dishonesty is when a student cheats or acts dishonestly in their schoolwork, exams, or research.

<2>t's broader than plagiarism—it includes any action that gives someone an unfair advantage or misrepresents their work.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

The rules for AI-generated content are still evolving, but generally fall under ethical, legal, and practical guidelines.
